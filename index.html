Win = 3 points, draw = 1. In Best of 3 the loser of a game goes first in the next one. Ties in a tournament are broken by opponents' match-win %, then game-win %, then at random. In Swiss, pairings avoid rematches when possible and the bye counts as a win. The ranking averages every simulated tournament: with the heuristic AI, decks that need long-term planning (engines built on structures and resources) are underrated.
